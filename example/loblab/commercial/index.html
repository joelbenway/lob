Frequently Asked Questions

How is headcount measured?

Headcount is measured by the total number of employees (full-time and part-time) across your organization and affiliates on the Effective Date of purchase. Under Section 4.3 of our COMMERCIAL.md, there are no upgrade fees if your team grows later.

What proof of license do I receive?

Upon checkout, Stripe immediately issues an official, itemized PDF receipt and invoice identifying 4th Box LLC as Licensor and your designated name or company as Licensee. That receipt, combined with the timeless terms in COMMERCIAL.md, constitutes your legally binding proof of commercial license.

Can I pay via Bank Wire, ACH, or Purchase Order?

Yes! For corporate accounting, purchase orders, ACH, or bank wires, contact us at lob@benway.me. We will promptly generate an official invoice with payment instructions.

Where can I read the full legal agreement?

The complete, binding agreement is published in the repository at COMMERCIAL.md. For open-source use under the copyleft license, see COPYING.md.
